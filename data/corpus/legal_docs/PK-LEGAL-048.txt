2026 M L D 935

[Punjab Consumer Court]

CC No. 1786/2026
Muhammad Asif Cheema … Complainant
versus
MetroCom Cellular (Pvt.) Ltd. … Respondent

Complaint under Section 25 of the Punjab Consumer Protection Act, 2005.
The complainant purchased a split air-conditioner
from the respondent's authorized dealer for a sale consideration of
Rs. 44000/-. The unit became defective within the
warranty period; the respondent refused replacement on the ground that
the defect was caused by user negligence — a claim unsupported by any
expert report. The Court holds that under Section 14 of the Act the
respondent is liable for breach of the implied warranty of fitness.
Replacement unit is ordered, plus compensation of Rs. 25,000/-. COMPLAINT ALLOWED.
