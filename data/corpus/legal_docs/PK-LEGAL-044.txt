2026 P Cr. L J 419

[High Court — Regular Second Appeal]

R.S.A. No. 6010/2024
Nasir Hussain Qureshi … Appellant
versus
Saeed Anwar Chaudhry … Respondent

This regular second appeal under Section 100 CPC arises out of the suit
for specific performance of agreement to sell dated [date] in respect of
4 kanals of agricultural land in Mauza
Mohra Noori. The
appellant paid earnest money of Rs. 1200000/- and
is willing and able to perform his part of the contract. The defendant
denies execution of the agreement. The trial court decreed the suit; the
appellate court reversed. The substantial questions of law include the
admissibility of the agreement under Article 79 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat
Order, 1984. Following "Hafiz Tassaduq Hussain v. Muhammad Din"
PLD 2011 SC 241, the appeal is ALLOWED, the appellate decree is set
aside and the trial court decree is restored.
