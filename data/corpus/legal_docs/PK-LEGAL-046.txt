2024 P L D 359

[High Court — Criminal Appellate Jurisdiction]

Cr. Appeal No. 780/2025
The State … Appellant
versus
Waqar Younis Cheema … Respondent

This is a State appeal against the judgment of acquittal passed by the
learned Sessions Judge in case FIR No. 142/2024
registered for an offence under Section 302 PPC at P.S.
Civil Lines. The prosecution relied on
ocular account of two eye-witnesses and recovery of crime weapon. The
trial court disbelieved the eye-witnesses on grounds of improbability of
their presence and disbelieved the recovery on account of long delay.
The principles governing interference with an acquittal are settled. An
acquittal carries a double presumption of innocence and ought not to be
disturbed unless found to be perverse, artificial or based on misreading.
See "Muhammad Mansha v. The State" 2018 SCMR 772. The findings of the
trial court are neither perverse nor against the record. APPEAL DISMISSED.
