Before: Mr. Justice Ijaz ul Ahsan

W.P. No. 9168/2026
Mehboob Alam Malik … Petitioner
versus
Federation of Pakistan through Secretary, Establishment Division, and others … Respondents

Counsel for petitioner: Mr. Shoaib Shaheen, Advocate
Counsel for respondents: Mr. Mian Bilal Hassan, Standing Counsel.

Subject: Constitutional petition under Article 199 of the Constitution of the
Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973 challenging the order of dismissal from
service dated [date], passed by the PIA.

JUDGMENT

Through this writ petition the petitioner, who was serving as GD-VI Cabin Crew in the
PIA, has impugned the order of his dismissal from service. The principal
ground is that the impugned order has been passed in violation of the
Government Servants (Efficiency and Discipline) Rules, 1973, in particular
Rule 5 thereof, and the doctrine of audi alteram partem.

2. Briefly, the facts are that the petitioner was appointed against the
post of GD-VI Cabin Crew approximately 22 years before the impugned
action and had an unblemished service record. A show-cause notice was
served upon him allowing only 7 days to reply. The reply
was filed within time. However, an inquiry officer was appointed and
proceeded to conduct the inquiry without supplying the petitioner with
copies of the documents relied upon by the prosecution and without affording
him an opportunity of personal hearing or cross-examination of witnesses.
The inquiry report was not supplied to the petitioner before the impugned
order of dismissal was passed.

3. Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that the impugned order is
violative of Rule 5 of the E&D Rules, 1973, the principle of natural
justice, and Articles 4, 9, 10-A and 25 of the Constitution. He places
reliance on the law laid down in "Government of Punjab v. Aamir Zahoor-ul-Haq"
PLD 2016 SC 421 and "Wapda v. Mahmood Ahmed" 2018 SCMR 1108.

4. Learned Standing Counsel, on the other hand, supports the impugned order
and contends that the petitioner had a remedy of departmental appeal which
was availed and rejected.

5. We have heard the parties and examined the record. The fundamental
infirmity in the impugned proceedings is that the inquiry report was not
supplied to the petitioner before the imposition of the major penalty. It
is settled law that non-supply of the inquiry report vitiates the
proceedings and renders the resultant order void. Mehboob Alam Malik's service of
22 years stood erased on the strength of an inquiry the
documents whereof were never put to him.

6. For the reasons aforesaid, this petition is ALLOWED. The impugned order
of dismissal dated [date] is set aside. The petitioner is reinstated in
service with all consequential benefits. The respondents shall, however,
be at liberty to proceed afresh in accordance with law and after due
observance of the principles of natural justice.

WRIT ALLOWED.
