2024 M L D 489

[High Court — Constitutional Jurisdiction]

Cr. Misc. No. 2901/2026
Waqar Younis Khan … Petitioner
versus
The State and others … Respondents

Constitutional petition under Article 199 read with Section 561-A,
Cr.P.C. 1898 for quashment of FIR No. 753 of 2024
registered at P.S. Sadar for an
offence under Section 489-F PPC. The petitioner contends that the dispute
is purely civil; cheques in question were issued as security and not
against any consideration. Following "Mehmood Akhtar Naqvi v. Federation
of Pakistan" PLD 2013 SC 195 and "Younas Khan v. The State" 2017 P.Cr.LJ 1145,
the FIR is QUASHED and the petitioner is at liberty to seek civil remedies.
