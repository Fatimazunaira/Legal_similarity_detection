Subject: First Appeal from Order under Section 47 of the Guardians and Wards

in petition for custody of a minor daughter aged 13 years.

JUDGMENT

This first appeal from order assails the judgment passed by the learned
Guardian Judge whereby custody of a minor daughter aged 13 years has been entrusted to the mother.
The principal ground urged is that the learned Family Court has misdirected
itself in law and on facts and has failed to appreciate the welfare
principle which is the polestar in matters of custody.

2. Briefly, the facts are that the parties are former spouses; their
marriage was dissolved through a decree of khula. The minors were the
issue of the said marriage. After the dissolution, a dispute arose as to
custody. The factual matrix shows that the daughter prefers her mother.

3. Learned counsel for the appellant urges that the welfare of a minor daughter aged 13 years
lies with him; that the appellant is gainfully employed; that suitable
residential accommodation is available; and that the impugned judgment is
based on conjecture rather than evidence.

4. The cardinal principle in custody disputes is the welfare of the minor.
Welfare is a composite concept comprising moral, educational, emotional and
physical well-being. The wishes of the parents, while relevant, are
subordinate to the welfare of the minor. Reliance is placed on
"Mst. Khalida Parveen v. Muhammad Sultan" PLD 2005 SC 519 and
"Mst. Razia Rehman v. Sajjad Ahmad" 2019 SCMR 1815.

5. Having gone through the record we are of the considered view that the
learned Guardian Judge has correctly weighed the welfare considerations.
The minors of tender years ordinarily reside with the mother in terms of
hizanat under Muslim Personal Law, subject to the welfare principle. The
findings of the learned trial court are not perverse and call for no
interference.

6. Resultantly, this first appeal is DISMISSED. The visitation schedule
fixed by the learned trial court shall continue. The appellant shall be at
liberty to seek modification of the schedule before the trial court, on
proof of changed circumstances.

APPEAL DISMISSED.
