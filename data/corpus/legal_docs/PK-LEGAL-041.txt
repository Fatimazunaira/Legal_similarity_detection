2025 M L D 941

[Family Court — Suit for Recovery of Dowry Articles]

F.A.O. No. 3158/2024
Nasir Hussain Khan … Plaintiff/Appellant
versus
Omar Farooq Bhatti … Defendant/Respondent

This appeal is directed against the judgment and decree of the learned
Family Court rejecting the appellant's claim for recovery of dowry
articles. The list of articles, duly stamped at the time of nikah, is
exhibited as Mark "P/1". The principal question is whether the trial
court was justified in placing the burden of proof on the appellant in a
case where the articles, by their very nature, remain in the matrimonial
home. Following "Muhammad Habib v. Mst. Safia Bibi" 2014 SCMR 1310, the
burden in such cases shifts to the husband to show that the articles were
returned. The judgment of the trial court is set aside; decree for
recovery of articles, or in the alternative, their market value, is
granted. APPEAL ALLOWED.
