2025 S C M R 1072

[High Court — Civil Revision]

C.R. No. 3634/2026
Omar Farooq Khan (Landlord) … Petitioner
versus
Rashid Latif Awan (Tenant) … Respondent

Civil revision under Section 115 CPC against the order of the Rent
Controller directing the petitioner to deposit rent at the tenanted rate
under Section 17 of the Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009. The grounds of
ejectment urged are personal bona fide need and default in payment of
rent for 6 consecutive months. The settled position
is that the bona fide need of the landlord, once established by
trustworthy evidence, is a sufficient ground. Following "Imtiaz Ahmad v.
Ghulam Ali" PLD 2002 SC 87, the revision is ALLOWED and the tenant is
directed to vacate the demised premises within sixty days.
