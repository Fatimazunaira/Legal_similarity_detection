2026 P Cr. L J 184

[High Court — Regular Second Appeal]

R.S.A. No. 6748/2024
Junaid Iqbal Khan (Daughter of late Hajji Abdul Karim) … Appellant
versus
Ahmad Raza Sandhu (Son of late Hajji Abdul Karim) … Respondent

Suit for declaration regarding inheritance of 10 kanals
of agricultural land left by the deceased father. The appellant, a daughter,
was excluded from the mutation of inheritance entered in the revenue
record. Such exclusion is a continuing wrong; limitation does not run
against an heir. Following "Ghulam Ali v. Mst. Ghulam Sarwar Naqvi"
PLD 1990 SC 1 and "Mst. Suba v. Sardar Ali" 2020 SCMR 1404, the appeal
is ALLOWED. The mutation excluding the appellant is declared void.
The Revenue Authorities are directed to enter the appellant's share in
accordance with the Sharʿiah-prescribed shares — daughter receiving half
the share of a son. APPEAL ALLOWED.
