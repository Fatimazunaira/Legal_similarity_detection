2026 P L D 652

[Election Tribunal — Punjab]

Election Petition No. 3457/2026
Mehboob Alam Abbasi … Petitioner
versus
Tariq Bashir Sandhu (Returned Candidate) and others … Respondents

This election petition under Section 139 of the Elections Act, 2017
challenges the result of the General Elections for Provincial Assembly
Constituency PP-174. Allegations include illegal
inclusion of bogus votes on the polling rolls, unlawful interference by
local administration, and stuffing of ballot boxes at polling station
nos. 78, 31 and 10.
The petitioner has produced Forms 45 and 46 along with the affidavits of
polling agents. After recording evidence, the Tribunal directs partial
recount under Section 95 of the Act. ORDER ACCORDINGLY.
