2025 P Cr. L J 55

[High Court — Bail Petition (NAB)]

Cr. Misc. (Bail) No. 3343/2024
Faisal Mehmood Awan … Petitioner
versus
The State through Chairman NAB … Respondent

Post-arrest bail petition in NAB Reference No. 261/2025
involving allegations of corruption and corrupt practices under Section
9(a)(iv), (vi) and (xii) of the National Accountability Ordinance, 1999.
The alleged misappropriation aggregates to Rs. 671 million.
After the 26th Constitutional Amendment and the judgment in
"Muhammad Akram Khan v. NAB" PLD 2024 SC 100, NAB courts retain bail
jurisdiction subject to ordinary principles. The petitioner is a sitting
member of District Council of
KPK and has been in
custody for 339 days. Trial has not commenced.
Following the principle that prolonged pre-trial detention without a
foreseeable conclusion of trial entitles the accused to bail
(see "Tallat Ishaq v. NAB" PLD 2019 SC 112), bail is GRANTED on furnishing
bonds of Rs. 5,000,000/-. PETITION ALLOWED.
