[High Court — Habeas Corpus Jurisdiction]

W.P. (Habeas) No. 8748/2024
Petition under Article 199(1)(b)(i) of the Constitution for production of
Faisal Mehmood Abbasi alleged to be detained in illegal confinement.

The petitioner, mother of the detenu, alleges that her son was picked up
on the night intervening 24/4 by
unknown persons and his whereabouts have remained unknown despite
repeated approaches to the local police. A Bailiff was deputed under
Section 491 Cr.P.C. who reported that the detenu was, in fact, in the
custody of District Police
without any formal arrest record. The detenu is forthwith produced and
released. Direction is issued to the SHO concerned to register an FIR if
any cognisable offence is made out. Compensatory costs of Rs. 50,000/-
are imposed on the detaining authority. PETITION ALLOWED.
